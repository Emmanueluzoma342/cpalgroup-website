Why Atuma-Iga

Three facts you can check without asking us.

The Second Niger Bridge is finished. A 1.63km crossing built between 2018 and 2023 at a cost of ₦336 billion, with 46.9km of link roads connecting Asaba to Onitsha. It is not a proposal. You can drive on it.

The road to the estate is a live government contract. Delta State Ministry of Works, Rural and Riverine Roads. Contractor RCT Nigeria Ltd. The project board is standing at Atuma-Iga and photographed on this page.

Other developers have already moved here. Atuma-Iga now carries several estates from different companies. When multiple developers independently commit to the same location, that tells you more than any brochure can.

We are not going to quote you a return, a percentage or a projected value. Nobody can honestly promise those. What we can do is point at things that already exist and let you draw your own conclusion.
