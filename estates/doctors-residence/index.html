Why Atuma-Iga

Asaba is expanding in one direction, and this is it.

Land does not become valuable because a company says so. It becomes valuable when roads, power and people arrive. All three are arriving here, and you can verify every line below without speaking to us.

A 1.63km crossing built between 2018 and 2023 at a cost of ₦336 billion, with 46.9km of link roads joining Asaba to Onitsha. When it opened, the federal works minister noted publicly that property values on both sides had risen. That is a matter of public record.

Your access road is a live government contract

Power and lighting are already installed

The questions people actually ask

Before you send anybody money.

Can I buy without coming to Nigeria?

Yes. We inspect the plot with you on a live video call in your own time zone, you direct the camera, and you can record the call. Documents are sent to you and to your lawyer for review before any payment. Many of our buyers complete this way.

What documents do I actually receive?

A Deed of Assignment and a registered survey plan, both in your name, issued on allocation. Your plot beacons are shown to you physically on site. The estate-wide Certificate of Occupancy is still being processed, and we say so plainly rather than letting you discover it later.

Why is your land cheaper than the estate next door?

Two reasons, both honest. We are a young company building a reputation in Asaba and we price accordingly. And estates that already hold a full Certificate of Occupancy reasonably charge more than we do, because ours is still in progress. You are buying earlier in the process, at a lower price.

Is the land free from dispute?

Yes, and you do not have to take our word for it. The Delta State government has a road project running to the estate with its board standing on site. Governments do not commit contracts to land in dispute. Bring your own surveyor or lawyer on inspection day if you prefer.

What happens if I cannot finish paying in six months?

A penalty of 5 percent of the property price applies for each month past the deadline, which is ₦225,000 a month. It is a serious figure and that is why we state it here rather than in small print. If six months looks tight for your situation, tell us before you pay the deposit and we will be straight with you about whether this is right for you.

Can I get my money back if I change my mind?

Yes. Your payment is refunded less a 20 percent administrative fee. Allow three weeks for processing. The terms are written into the agreement you sign before any money moves.

How do I know I am paying the real company?

CPAL account details are confirmed to you directly by our office, on a recorded call or in writing. Never act on account details sent through a WhatsApp status, a group chat or a forwarded flyer, even from a realtor you know. Call 0806 789 8622 and check. This protects you from us as much as from anybody else.

Is this an investment with a guaranteed return?

No, and any company telling you otherwise is one to walk away from. We sell land as ownership. What we can show you is what already exists here: a completed bridge, a government road contract, installed power, and other developers who arrived before us. What you conclude from that is your own judgement to make.
