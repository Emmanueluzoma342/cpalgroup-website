Read this part slowly

If you are sending money home for land, you already know somebody it happened to.

Paid for a plot from abroad. Sent the money to a number somebody vouched for. Got a receipt, then a photograph, then excuses, then silence. Or worse, got the land and found out four years later that the papers never existed.

That is not a rare story in Nigerian real estate. It is common enough that many people in the diaspora have simply decided to wait until they can come home and stand on the land themselves.

We think that is a reasonable decision, and we have built our process around it. You do not have to trust us. You have to be able to check us.

The location, checked

Atuma-Iga is not a guess. It is where other developers already went.

The Second Niger Bridge is complete. A 1.63km crossing built between 2018 and 2023 at a cost of ₦336 billion, with 46.9km of link roads connecting Asaba to Onitsha. When it opened, the federal works minister noted publicly that property values on both sides had risen sharply. That is a matter of record, not a sales claim.

Your access road is a live government contract. Delta State Ministry of Works, Rural and Riverine Roads, contractor RCT Nigeria Ltd. The board stands on site. Photograph it yourself on inspection day.

Atuma-Iga now carries several estates from different companies. Independent developers do not commit capital to the same location by coincidence. That convergence is worth more than any brochure paragraph.

What the default penalty actually costs.
Five percent of ₦4,500,000 is ₦225,000 for every month you run past the six-month deadline. That is a serious number and we are putting it in front of you rather than in small print. If six months is tight for your situation, tell us before you pay the deposit, not after.
The flexibility is real, though.
There is no fixed monthly instalment. Pay ₦500,000 to begin, then pay what you can, when you can, until the balance clears. For anyone whose income arrives in irregular amounts, that is easier to live with than a fixed debit date.

All of this appears in the agreement you sign before any money moves. Read it. Ask about anything that is not clear.

The questions people actually ask

Before you send anybody money.

  1. Can I buy without coming to Nigeria?

    Yes. Video inspection, documents sent to you and to your lawyer for review, payment to the corporate account, documentation issued in your name. Many buyers do it this way. Nobody is asked to skip a step because of distance.

  2. What does "C of O in view" actually mean?

    The estate-wide Certificate of Occupancy is being processed and has not been issued. Your Deed of Assignment and registered survey plan are what you hold in the meantime, and they are real, registered documents. We would rather tell you this plainly than have you discover it later.

  3. Who exactly do I pay?

    Chuks Properties Academy Limited, corporate account only. Not a realtor. Not a representative. Not a number sent to you in a WhatsApp status. Call the office on 0806 789 8622 and confirm the details with us directly before you send anything.

  4. What if I pay and then need to withdraw?

    You are refunded your full payment less 20 percent, which is the administrative fee. Allow three weeks for the refund to be processed. This is written into the agreement you sign, so you will have read it before you commit.

  5. Can my family member inspect for me?

    Yes. Send whoever you trust. We will walk them through the same inspection, and you can join the call while they are on site.

  6. How many plots are left?

    Phase 1 runs along the road and holds 64 plots. Twenty have been sold. Forty-four remain. We update this page as that changes.
