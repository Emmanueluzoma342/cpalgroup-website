The questions we actually get asked

Straight answers.

What document do I get, and whose name is on it?

A registered survey plan and a deed of assignment, both issued in your own name. Ask any seller this question. If the answer takes more than one sentence, be careful.

I live abroad. Can I still buy?

Yes. We walk the land on a live video call with you, at a time that suits your timezone. You choose where we point the camera. If you would rather not rely on us at all, send a cousin, a friend or your own surveyor and we will meet them at the gate.

Do I have to pay anything to hold a plot?

No. Nothing is paid before you have stood on the land or seen it on video. No deposit, no holding fee.

What if I cannot finish paying in six months?

The penalty is 5 percent of the property price for each month past six months. It is written into your terms in plain language rather than buried in small print. If six months looks tight for your situation, tell us before you pay the deposit and we will talk it through.
